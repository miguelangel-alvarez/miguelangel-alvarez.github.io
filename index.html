Arduino Attitude Monitoring System

Personal Project | February 2026 – March 2026 | Arduino, MPU6050

A real-time 3-axis attitude monitor built with an MPU6050 and a 16×2 LCD on an Elegoo Uno R3, sampling ωx, ωy, and ωz at 1 Hz.

  • Models the sensor as a rigid body and displays torques, kinetic energy, and angular momentum across three body axes in real time
  • Identified gyroscope saturation at the default ±250°/s range, which shows the range requirements for attitude control in propulsion-driven maneuvers
